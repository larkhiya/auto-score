Administrative Assistant, Operations Team (fictional company: Harrow & Vale Property Services)

About the role
We are looking for an Administrative Assistant to keep a busy 15-person operations team organised. You will be the first point of contact for the office and make sure meetings, documents and records run smoothly.

What you will do
- Manage calendars for three managers, schedule meetings and book rooms, travel and accommodation.
- Answer the main phone line and the shared inbox, and direct enquiries to the right person.
- Prepare letters, reports and presentations in Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint (or Google Workspace).
- Keep digital and paper records accurate and up to date, including contracts and supplier files.
- Process purchase orders, invoices and expense claims and track them in a spreadsheet.
- Take minutes at weekly team meetings and follow up on action points.
- Order office supplies and coordinate with building maintenance.

What we are looking for
- At least two years of experience in an administrative, office or reception role.
- Strong organisation skills and attention to detail.
- Confident with Microsoft Office or Google Workspace, especially Excel.
- Clear written and spoken English.
- You must be able to work in our office four days a week.

Nice to have
- Experience with a project or task tool such as Asana or Trello.
- Experience supporting a property, facilities or real-estate business.
- A recognised administrative or office-management qualification.

What we offer
Hybrid working (one day from home), 25 days of annual leave, a training budget and a friendly team.
